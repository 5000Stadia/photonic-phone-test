# Packed crowd hair

Derived from the already-packed assets in `spikes/mhhair/data/`; original notices are in `vendor/makehuman-hair/NOTICE.md`.

- bob: Margaret Toigo, CC0; http://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/1685
- long: Elvaerwyn, CC BY; http://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/2816
- braid: MakeHuman system assets, CC0; http://www.makehumancommunity.org
- afro: MakeHuman system assets, CC0; http://www.makehumancommunity.org
- updo: Elvaerwyn, CC BY; http://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/2001
- crop: MakeHuman system assets, CC0; http://www.makehumancommunity.org

Modified for the people renderer: age-specific body bindings, collar clearance, coarse far topology and short flyaways. Rebuild with `node tools/people/hair-pack.mjs`. Crop uses the available v1 shell; the other five use v2.
